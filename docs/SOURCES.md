# Accuracy, sources and credits

The facts in the app (part locations, fluids and capacities, bulb types, warning-light meanings, tire sizes
and pressures, maintenance codes, common problems) come from:

- **Honda:** the 2012 and 2013 Civic sedan specification sheets and press kits on
  [hondanews.com](https://hondanews.com) and [hondanews.ca](https://hondanews.ca), the Honda R&D Technical
  Review paper on the R18 engine, the Civic owner's manuals (2012 sedan, 2013 coupe, 2013 Hybrid sedan, 2014
  sedan) and the genuine Honda parts catalog;
- **[NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov):** crash-test measurement reports, safety ratings and service bulletins;
- **repair guides and videos:** [CarCareKiosk](https://www.carcarekiosk.com), [1A Auto](https://www.1aauto.com),
  [Paul's Travel Pictures](https://www.paulstravelpictures.com), [iFixit](https://www.ifixit.com) and
  [Instructables](https://www.instructables.com), each credited next to its link in the app;
- **owner reports:** [CarComplaints](https://www.carcomplaints.com) and [TrueDelta](https://www.truedelta.com).

Every fact is written up with its source and a confidence level in [docs/research/](research/README.md).
Where sources disagree (for example the recommended tire pressure), the app says so and tells you to check the
label on your own car.
