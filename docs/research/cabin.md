## SUMMARY (key facts)

- **Dash:**
  - Two-tier instrument panel. The upper tier has a **digital speedometer** and the **i-MID** (a 5-inch LCD on the LX). The lower tier, behind the steering wheel, has an **analog tachometer**.
  - There is **no coolant temperature gauge**, only High (red) and Low (blue) temperature indicator lights.
  - Green **ECON** button. "Eco Assist" ambient bars beside the speedometer change from green to blue-green to blue.
- **LX equipment for 2013:** rearview camera (shown on the i-MID), Bluetooth HandsFreeLink, Pandora, SMS texting, USB, a 160 W / 4-speaker CD stereo, manual A/C (automatic climate control is EX/EX-L only), cruise control, cloth seats, manual driver-seat height adjustment, front, side and side-curtain airbags, and TPMS.
- **Shifter (5AT):** **P-R-N-D-D3-2-1**, console-mounted. The parking brake is a hand lever with a release button.
- **TPMS:** the 2013 Civic uses a **direct, sensor-in-wheel TPMS**. There is no owner calibration button; that button arrived on the 2014–15 Civic.
- **Cabin ("dust and pollen") filter:**
  - Location: behind the glove box. Squeeze the glove box sides to drop it, unclip the filter cover, and fit the new filter with the arrow pointing **down**.
  - Replacement: Maintenance Minder **sub-item 2**, or every 15,000 mi in heavy-soot urban driving.
  - Parts: OEM 80292-SDA-407, about 9.5 × 9.7 × 1.3 in.
- **Fuses:**
  - The interior box is under the dashboard on the driver's side, with the fuse map on a side-panel label.
  - The engine-bay box is next to the brake fluid reservoir; the fuse puller is clipped to the back of its cover.
- **Release levers:**
  - Hood: under the lower-left corner of the dash.
  - Fuel door: at the foot of the driver's seat.
  - Trunk: to the lower left of the driver's seat, and on the key remote.
- **Bulbs:**
  - Low beam HB4 (9006, 51 W), high beam HB3 (9005, 60 W), both changed from the engine bay.
  - Front turn/park/side marker: 28/8 W amber.
  - Brake/tail: 21/5 W. Rear turn: 21 W amber.
  - Sedan rear side marker: LED. Trunk-lid taillight: 3 CP. Back-up: 16 W.
  - License plate: 5 W. High-mount brake light: LED.
  - Map and ceiling lights: 8 W.
  - Rear bulbs are changed from inside the trunk, behind the trim lining.
- **Wipers:** 26 in driver / 22 in passenger (sedan). Honda gives no time interval; replace when the blades streak.

- **Meanings and actions** come from the 2013 Civic Coupe owner's manual, Indicators section, printed pages 64–74 (viewer: https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/240857/honda-2013-civic-coupe.html?page=65 to `?page=75`), plus the "Handling the Unexpected" pages 305–310. These are **[Honda manual]**.

- **Beginner tip for the model:** at key-ON, almost every warning light comes on for a few seconds as a bulb check, then goes off once the engine starts. That is normal.

### 7b. Headlight bulb replacement (from the engine bay)

- Honda's steps (2013 OM-Coupe p.262–265; the 2014 OM-Sedan p.377–379 is identical for the 1.8 L):
  1. **Driver side, 1.8 L:** remove the holding clips (pull straight up), push up the tab, and remove the air-intake-duct cover and its upper part to make room.
  2. **Passenger side:** pry up the center pin of clip (C) with a flat screwdriver and lift off the upper part of the windshield-washer reservoir.
  3. Push the tab and disconnect the coupler (connector) from the bulb.
  4. Turn the old bulb **counterclockwise** and remove it.
  5. Insert the new bulb and turn it **clockwise** to lock it.
  6. Reconnect the coupler and refit the clips.
- High-beam bulbs use the same tab, coupler and twist procedure.
- **Don't touch the glass of a halogen bulb.** Skin oil or a scratch can make it overheat and shatter. If you touch it, clean it with denatured alcohol.
- Sources: https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/240857/honda-2013-civic-coupe.html?page=263 to `?page=266`; https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=379 to `?page=381`. **[Honda manual]**
- Independent confirmation: iFixit shows access from under the hood, unclipping the washer-fluid part on one side, and turning the bulb counterclockwise (https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/2012+2013+2014+2015+Honda+Civic+Headlight+Replacement/141069). **[multiple sources]**

### 7c. Front turn/parking bulb

- Access is through the **inner fender liner (wheel well)**, not the engine bay:
  1. Turn the steering wheel fully the opposite way.
  2. Remove the liner clips and pull the liner back.
  3. Twist out the socket.
- Source: 2014 OM-Sedan p.383 (https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=385). **[Honda manual]**

### 7d. Taillight / brake / rear turn / back-up / license bulbs (from the trunk)

- **Body-side lamps** (brake/tail, rear turn):
  1. Inside the trunk, pry out the holding clips with a flat screwdriver and pull the trunk-side lining back.
  2. Turn the socket counterclockwise and remove it.
  3. Swap the bulb.
  - Source: 2014 OM-Sedan p.385. **[Honda manual]**
- **Trunk-lid lamps** (inner taillight 3 CP, back-up 16 W):
  1. Remove the screw in the center of the fastener (Phillips), pry the clips and pull the **trunk-lid lining** back.
  2. Turn the socket counterclockwise.
  3. Swap the bulb.
  - Source: 2014 OM-Sedan p.386. **[Honda manual]**
- **License plate light:**
  1. Remove the trunk-lid lining as above.
  2. Squeeze the tabs on the socket to remove the assembly.
  3. Swap the bulb.
  - Source: 2014 OM-Sedan p.387. **[Honda manual]**
- iFixit (2012–15 Civic) confirms rear bulbs are changed from inside the trunk: remove the trunk liner fasteners and turn the socket a quarter turn counterclockwise. Its intro says to open the trunk "using the latch on the driver's side floor". **[multiple sources]**

## Things that are uncertain or not found

1. **The 2013 Sedan manual itself was not accessed.** The 2013 Coupe manual (same year and powertrain) and the 2014 Sedan manual (same body) were used instead.
2. **Indicator colours** are not stated in the 2013 manual text. Colours come from Honda UK, Honda AU and SlashGear generic guides. ABS and EPS conflict between sources (amber vs red). On a US 2013 Civic these are most likely amber, but **look at the actual cluster at key-ON** to confirm for the 3D model.
3. **TPMS type:** direct (sensor) TPMS is concluded from the Honda parts catalog, the Las Positas relearn chart and the manual. Tire Review's article disagrees.
4. **Fuel gauge** position (upper or lower tier) and the exact position of the **ECON button** were not confirmed in text.
5. **Washer nozzle** mounting (hood or cowl) was not confirmed.
6. Trade numbers for the small bulbs (front turn 28/8 W, taillight 3 CP, map/dome 8 W) vary across aftermarket charts; only the Honda wattages are firm.
7. Front accessory socket fuse No. 27 is 20 A in the coupe manual but 15 A for 2012–13 per fuse-box.info. Trust the label in the car.
