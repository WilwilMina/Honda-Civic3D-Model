- **[Honda]**: Honda press/spec sheet, Honda owner's manual, or Honda service information.

## 1. Brakes

1.1 **Front:** power-assisted ventilated discs. On the LX the size depends on the transmission: **11.1 in on the 5-speed automatic** and 10.3 in on the 5-speed manual. [Honda] [spec sheet](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications). The 2013 refresh increased the front rotor "20 mm from 262 to 282 mm" on automatic LX, EX and EX-L models. [Honda press release, reprinted] [conceptcarz](https://www.conceptcarz.com/vehicle/z22272/concepts.aspx)
- The OE rotor is 45251-TA5-A00 (shared with Accord 4-cyl models), a 282 mm vented rotor with 5 holes. [Honda-cat / Single] [hondapartsnow front brake](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/front_brake_1_8l.html), [hybrid-racing](https://www.hybrid-racing.com/collections/brakes/products/brembo-uv-coated-front-rotor-for-03-17-accord-06-15-civic). Rotor thickness and minimum thickness: **not found**.
- Each rotor is held to the hub by two flat screws (6x14). [Honda-cat, same diagram] The guide says these are Phillips screws. [Single] [Paul's](https://www.paulstravelpictures.com/2012-2015-Honda-Civic-Front-Brake-Pads-Replacement-Guide/)

1.2 **Caliper:** **single-piston sliding (floating) caliper**. [Uncertain, but strongly indicated] Evidence: the catalog lists one "Piston" per caliper (qty 2 for 2 calipers), plus "Pin, Guide" and "Pin, Lock" slide pins. [Honda-cat] [diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/front_brake_1_8l.html). A guide on a 2013 LX sedan also shows two guide pins, 12 mm caliper bolts and 17 mm bracket-to-knuckle bolts. [Single] [Paul's](https://www.paulstravelpictures.com/2012-2015-Honda-Civic-Front-Brake-Pads-Replacement-Guide/)
- Pads have a metal squeal-type wear indicator; on the 2013 LX it sits on the top of the inner pad. [Single] [Paul's](https://www.paulstravelpictures.com/2012-2015-Honda-Civic-Front-Brake-Pads-Replacement-Guide/). Pad thickness and service limit: **not found**.
- Front splash guard (dust shield) part name "Splash Guard, FR. (15")". [Honda-cat]

1.3 **Rear (LX): drum brakes, 7.9 in.** [Honda] [spec](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications). Only the EX and EX-L have rear discs (10.2 in solid). [Honda]
- Parts in each drum: drum, 2 shoes, wheel cylinder, backing plate, 2 return springs (A/B), and adjuster bolt, lever and spring. [Honda-cat] [rear drum diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/rear_brake_drum.html). The adjuster lever and spring point to **self-adjusting** drums. [Uncertain, inferred from the parts]
- Drum inside diameter 200 mm, maximum (discard) 201 mm. [Single, aftermarket drum spec] [PowerStop JBD1003P](https://finditparts.com/products/15603330/powerstop-brakes-jbd1003p)

1.4 **ABS, EBD (electronic brake distribution), Brake Assist, VSA with traction control, and TPMS** are standard on every 2013 sedan trim. [Honda] [spec](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications). There is a "VSA Modulator" diagram. [Honda-cat] [index](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis.html). Each wheel has a speed sensor ("Sensor Assy." front and rear). [Honda-cat] [knuckle](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/knuckle.html), [rear lower arm](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/rear_lower_arm.html)

1.5 **Brake fluid:** "Honda Heavy Duty Brake Fluid DOT 3". [Honda] [2014 OM spec page](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=447). The 2012 Civic service info adds that DOT 3 or DOT 4 from a sealed container is a temporary substitute and that DOT 5 is incompatible. [Honda] [2012 Civic HF service info](https://workshop-manuals.com/honda/civic_hf/l4-1.8l/brakes_and_traction_control/hydraulic_system_brakes/brake_fluid/component_information/specifications/)
- **Interval:** "replace the brake fluid every 3 years", independent of the Maintenance Minder. [Honda] [2014 OM p361](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=361)
- Maintenance Minder "B" includes: inspect front and rear brakes, check the parking brake adjustment, and inspect brake hoses and lines (including ABS/VSA). [Honda] [same page](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=361)
- Reservoir location: the guide says "right rear area of the engine bay", but its viewpoint is unclear. [Uncertain] [Paul's](https://www.paulstravelpictures.com/2012-2015-Honda-Civic-Front-Brake-Pads-Replacement-Guide/)

1.6 **Parking brake:** a hand-operated "Lever Assy., Parking Brake" with a switch (for the warning light), an equalizer, and two cables ("Wire B, R./L. Parking Brake") to the rear drums. [Honda-cat] [diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/parking_brake_drum.html). Cable-operated: [Honda-cat], inferred from the "wire" parts.

## 2. Suspension

2.1 **Front: MacPherson strut. Rear: multi-link. Stabilizer bars 19 mm front / 16 mm rear (all 2013 sedan trims).** [Honda] [spec](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications)

2.2 **2013 changes vs 2012:** stiffer front springs, a thicker front stabilizer bar with Teflon-lined bushings, a thicker rear bar, a higher rear spring rate, reworked rear bushings, and a **stiffer front subframe**. [Honda press release, reprinted] [conceptcarz](https://www.conceptcarz.com/vehicle/z22272/concepts.aspx)

2.3 **Front corner parts:** [Honda-cat] [front shock diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/front_shock_absorber_1_8l.html), [knuckle diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/knuckle.html)
- Strut ("Shock Absorber Unit"), coil spring, upper spring seat, lower spring rubber, mount rubber, mount bearing and cap.
- Knuckle, lower arm, and a lower ball joint with dust boot. The lower arm is held by a 12 mm nut ("Nut, Arm (LOWER)").
- A "Bush, FR. Compliance" (one per side), the stabilizer bar with holder bushings and brackets, and front stabilizer links (end links), one per side.
- Front hub: separate "Hub Assy." plus "Bearing Assy., FR. Hub", retained by a 78 mm inner circlip. This points to a press-in front bearing. [Honda-cat] [front brake diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/front_brake_1_8l.html); press-in is [Uncertain], inferred.

- Rear stabilizer bar with holder bushings and brackets, plus rear stabilizer links.
- Rear hub: a bolt-on "Bearing Assy., RR. Hub Unit". [Honda-cat] [rear drum diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/rear_brake_drum.html)

RR." with upper mounting rubber and lower seat rubber, and a separate "Shock Absorber Assy., RR." with a lower bush and mounting rubbers. [Honda-cat] [rear shock diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/rear_shock_absorber.html). ## 3. Steering

3.1 "Motion-Adaptive Electric Power-Assisted Rack-and-Pinion Steering (EPS)": **ratio 14.89, 2.87 turns lock-to-lock, turning diameter 35.4 ft curb-to-curb.** [Honda] [spec](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications). The 2013 refresh reworked the EPS "to reduce friction and provide a quicker ratio". [Honda reprint] [conceptcarz](https://www.conceptcarz.com/vehicle/z22272/concepts.aspx)

3.2 **The motor is on the rack.** The 2013 LX "P.S. Gear Box" diagram lists "Motor" (53602-TR0-A03), "O-Ring, Motor", "Rack, Power Steering", an EPS harness (LH), tie-rod ends and boots, and gearbox mounting bolts. [Honda-cat] [diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/p_s_gear_box.html). The steering column assembly is a separate, much cheaper part. [Honda-cat] [column](https://hondapartsnow.com/oem-2013-honda-civic-steering_column.html). Aftermarket listings also sell "Power Rack and Pinion With Steering Motor" for the 2013–2015 1.8. [Single] [partrequest](https://www.partrequest.com/auto-parts-detail/power-rack-and-pinion-with-steering-motor-1-8l-fits-164871249419). Exactly where on the rack the motor sits (concentric or pinion-side) was **not confirmed**.

3.3 The column has manual tilt and telescope adjustment on all 9th-gen Civics. [Honda] [2012 Civic overview](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-6d8989c9457f44b4aa13e154399dd31b-2012-honda-civic-overview). There is no hydraulic pump or fluid. EPS has "no parasitic power loss to the engine". [Honda, same page]

3.4 **EPS bulletins and recalls:**
- **AER15070B (2015): engineering investigation, not a repair.** It covers 2012–2015 Civics with the EPS light on, "with/without loss of power steering assist", and DTCs 33-01/33-02/33-06. [Honda via NHTSA] [NHTSA PDF](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2015/MC-10107323-9340.pdf), [recallcheck](https://recallcheck.net/tsb/68890)
- TSB **14-058**, the EPS warranty extension for DTC 32-09/61-04, appears to cover older Fit/Civic models, not the 2013. [Uncertain] [carcomplaints](https://www.carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2008/tsbs/tsb-14-058.shtml), [vehicleservicepros](https://vehicleservicepros.com/industry-news/article/53089920/honda-electric-power-steering-2015-10-28)
- No steering recall was found for the 2013 Civic. [Single] [carcomplaints recalls](https://carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/recalls)

## 4. Wheels and tires

4.1 **LX: 15 in wheels with full covers, P195/65 R15 89H all-season tires.** [Honda] [spec](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications)

4.2 **Steel wheel 42700-TR3-A02, 15 x 6½J (TPMS, made by Topy), for the 2013 2-door/4-door LX.** [Honda-cat] [hondapartsnow](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/oem-2013-honda-civic-rims.html). The 2014 OM also maps 15 x 6½J to the LX. [Honda] [2014 OM p447](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=447). Note that the 2012 overview says "15x6" for the 2012 DX/LX; the 6½J wheel applies to 2013. [Honda] [2012 overview](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-6d8989c9457f44b4aa13e154399dd31b-2012-honda-civic-overview)

4.3 **The wheel cover is held on by the wheel nuts.** The OM says the cover cannot come off until the nuts are removed, and warns not to pry it off with a screwdriver. [Honda] [2014 OM p417](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=417)

4.4 **Recommended pressure: [Uncertain].**
- The 2014 OM tire table pairs P195/65R15 89H (DX/LX) with **30 psi (210 kPa)** and HF/EX/EX-L with 32 psi. This comes from reading the page's footnote order. [Honda, inferred from layout] [2014 OM p447](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=447)
- tirepressure.com lists 32/32 psi for the 2013 LX. [Single] [tirepressure.com](https://tirepressure.com/2013-honda-civic-tire-pressure)
- The label is on the **driver's doorjamb**. [Honda] [2014 OM p394](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=394). **Read the label on your own car and use that number.**

4.5 **Wheel nut torque 80 lbf·ft (108 N·m, 11 kgf·m).** Tighten in a criss-cross order, going around 2–3 times. [Honda] [2014 OM p418](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=418). Do not add torque with a foot or a pipe. [Honda] [p417](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=417)

4.6 **Fasteners and hub:**
- 5 x 114.3 bolt pattern [Multi] [wheel-sizes bolt pattern](https://wheel-sizes.com/bolt-pattern/honda/civic/2013/), [wheel-sizes lugnuts](https://wheel-sizes.com/lugnuts/honda/civic/)

- 5 nuts per wheel [Single] [Paul's](https://www.paulstravelpictures.com/2012-2015-Honda-Civic-Front-Brake-Pads-Replacement-Guide/)
- The LX nut is 90381-SV1-981. [Honda-cat] [hondapartsnow lug nuts](https://hondapartsnow.com/oem-2013-honda-civic-lug_nuts.html). It is 19 mm hex. [Single] [eliteracefab](https://eliteracefab.com/products/genuine-oem-honda-single-wheel-lug-nut-with-retainer-90381-sv1-981-x4)
- The studs are press-in "Bolt, Wheel" parts in the hub. [Honda-cat] [front brake diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/chassis/front_brake_1_8l.html)

4.7 **Rotation:**

- Honda shows the pattern **only as a diagram**, with no text. The diagram could not be read, so the exact pattern (for example forward-cross) is **not verified**. [Uncertain]

4.8 Snow chains: front only. For P195/65R15 Honda specifies the SCC Radial Chain SC1030 cable type. [Honda] [2014 OM p401](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=401)

## 5. Trunk and spare

5.1 **Compact spare T135/80 D15 99M** on the LX (the HF gets a Tire Repair Kit instead). [Honda] [spec](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-152f07edcfbf4ebeadde2b4f7ab8c01e-2013-honda-civic-sedan-specifications). The spare wheel is 42700-TR0-A51, a 15 x 4T black steel Topy wheel listed for the 4-door LX; HF models list a "Temporary kit" instead. [Honda-cat] [spare wheel page](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/oem-2013-honda-civic-spare_wheel.html). The 2014 OM lists the same T135/80D15 99M on a 15 x 4T wheel. [Honda] [p447](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=447)

5.2 **Spare rules:** **60 psi (420 kPa)**, check it periodically, **stay under 50 mph (80 km/h)**, use only the spare made for this model, and never put chains on it. [Honda] [2014 OM p413](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=413). Fitting the spare turns on the TPMS light, and the i-MID shows "CHECK TPMS SYSTEM". This is normal. [Honda] [p419](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=419)

5.3 **Where things are:** the spare lies in a well **under the trunk floor lid**, held by a **wing bolt and spacer cone**. The jack and wheel nut wrench are in a **tool case in the trunk**. [Honda] [2014 OM p414](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=414), [2013 Civic Hybrid OM](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/240893/honda-2013-civic-hybrid.html?page=307) (same layout)

5.4 **Tool kit parts:** "Jack Assy., Pantograph" (a scissor jack), "Wrench, Wheel", and "Bar, Jack" (the jack handle bar). [Honda-cat] [tools diagram](https://www.hondapartsnow.com/parts-list/2013-honda-civic--4dr_lx-ka_5at/interior_bumper/tools_jack.html). The OM diagram labels "Jack", "Jack Handle Bar" and "Wrench as Jack Handle", meaning the wheel wrench doubles as the jack crank. [Honda] [2014 OM p416](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=416)

5.5 **Jacking points:**
- Use the jacking point **nearest the flat tire**. Wind the jack up until its top meets the jacking point, with the **jacking point tab sitting in the jack notch**. [Honda] [2014 OM p416](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=416), [2013 Hybrid OM p309](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/240893/honda-2013-civic-hybrid.html?page=309)
- **Exact positions were not found in text** (they appear only in OM images). The usual Honda layout is the side-sill pinch weld just behind each front wheel and just ahead of each rear wheel. [Uncertain, not verified]
- For a floor jack, the 2012 Civic service info names a "front jacking bracket" and a "rear jacking bracket". Jack stands go under the vehicle-lift support points (diagram only). [Honda] [workshop-manuals 2012 Civic HF](https://workshop-manuals.com/honda/civic_hf/l4-1.8l/maintenance/vehicle_lifting/component_information/service_and_repair/)

5.6 **Towing:** a flatbed is best. A wheel-lift that raises the front wheels is acceptable. Do not lift or tow by the bumpers or with a rope or chain. [Honda] [2014 OM p444](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=444). **Tow hook / tow eye: not found** in the OM page or in the Tools-Jack parts diagram.

5.7 **Changing a flat (summary of the 2014 Civic Sedan OM, pp. 412–419):**
1. Brake gradually and stop on firm, level, non-slippery ground. Set the parking brake, shift to P, turn on the hazards, and turn the ignition to LOCK. [p413](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=413)
2. Open the trunk floor lid, take out the tool case, and remove the jack and wheel nut wrench. [p414](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=414)
3. Unscrew the wing bolt, remove the spacer cone, and lift out the spare. [p414]
4. Put a wheel block or rock in front of and behind the wheel **diagonally opposite** the flat. [p414]
5. Lay the spare under the body near the flat, wheel side up. This is a safety backstop. [p415](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=415)
6. Loosen each wheel nut about **one turn** while the tire is still on the ground. [p415]
7. Place the jack under the nearest jacking point, seat the tab in the jack notch, and raise the car with the jack bar and wrench until the tire is off the ground. Never go under a car held only by the jack, and do not jack with people in the car. [p416](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=416)
8. Remove the nuts, the **wheel cover** (it comes off only after the nuts), and the flat. Wipe the mounting surfaces, mount the spare, and run the nuts on until they touch. [p417](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=417)
9. Lower the car and remove the jack. Tighten the nuts in a criss-cross order, 2–3 times around, to **80 lbf·ft (108 N·m)**. [p418](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=418)
10. Put the flat face-down in the spare well. Flip the spacer cone over on the wing bolt and secure the tire. Stow the tools. [p418]
11. Drive under 50 mph and get the full-size tire repaired or replaced soon. Recalibrate the TPMS after a regular tire goes back on. [p413], [p419](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/713284/Honda-Civic.html?page=419)

## 7. Common problems (with sources)

- **Recalls:** carcomplaints lists **no NHTSA recalls** for the 2013 Civic. [Single] [carcomplaints](https://carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/recalls). Owners should check their VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
- **EPS warning light / loss of assist:**
  - carcomplaints lists "Power Steering Warning Light On" (3 complaints, about $3,000 average at about 86k mi) and "EPS Stopped Working While Driving" (2). [Single] [steering](https://www.carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/steering/), [overview](https://www.carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/)

  - TrueDelta has a sedan report at 85k mi: the front passenger bearing buzzed at 40–60 mph, repaired for $450. [Single] [truedelta](https://www.truedelta.com/2013-Honda-Civic/suspension-problems-109)
- **Brakes:**
  - carcomplaints lists "brakes grinding" (4), "wearing out prematurely" (2) and "ABS light on" (1). [Single] [brakes](https://www.carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/brakes/)

- **Tires/wheels:** carcomplaints lists cheap OE tires (2), TPMS light (2), vibration (2) and hydroplaning (1). [Single] [wheels/hubs](https://www.carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/wheels_hubs/)
- **Suspension:** only 2 complaints on carcomplaints (stiff ride / motion sickness). [Single] [suspension](https://www.carcomplaints.com/Honda/Civic/2013/suspension/). TrueDelta has one report of a leaking rear shock at 63k mi. [Single] [truedelta](https://www.truedelta.com/2013-Honda-Civic/suspension-problems-109)
- **Rear drum adjusters and sway-bar end links:** **no 2013-specific evidence found** of these being common problems, so the app does not claim they are.

## 8. Conflicts and do-not-use notes

- The Honda "2012 Civic overview" page (https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-6d8989c9457f44b4aa13e154399dd31b-2012-honda-civic-overview) is very long and appears to include **older-generation history** further down. Those deeper sections give figures that **contradict** the 2013 spec sheet:
  - 16.1:1 ratio and 3.1 turns (the 2013 spec says 14.89 and 2.87)
  - 17/11 mm stabilizer bars (the 2013 spec says 19/16)
  - a rack-concentric motor, rear "upper A-arms / double lower tubular lateral links", 7.8 in drums, and a T135/80D16 spare (the 16-inch spare is the Si's)
  
  **Do not use those figures.** The 2013 spec sheet and the 2013 parts catalog take priority.
- The wheel width changed: 2012 DX/LX "15x6" vs the 2013 LX catalog 15 x 6½J.
- The tire pressure conflict (30 vs 32 psi): see §4.4.
