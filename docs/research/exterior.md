# 2013 Honda Civic LX Sedan (9th gen, US, FB2, R18Z1, 5AT): Exterior Reference for the 3D Model

Confidence tags:
- **[HONDA]**: a Honda source (hondanews.com / hondanews.ca spec sheet, Honda owner's manual, Honda parts catalog name or number)
- **[MULTI]**: two or more independent non-Honda sources agree
- **[SINGLE]**: one non-Honda source
- **[EST]**: an estimate. The method is given, and the number has not been measured from the car.

Units: Honda US publishes inches. Converted to mm (1 in = 25.4 mm) unless hondanews.ca gives an official mm figure, in which case that figure is shown.

## 1. Dimensions and 2012 vs 2013 differences

**2013 Civic Sedan (all US trims share the body dimensions)** [HONDA] ([hn13])
- Length 179.4 in, width 69.0 in, height 56.5 in, wheelbase 105.1 in, track 59.0 in front and 59.9 in rear. The rear track is **wider** than the front track by about 23 mm.
- Curb weight LX: 2740 lb (manual) / **2815 lb (automatic)**.
- Ground clearance is not in the US sheet. The Canadian press kit, which uses the same North American body, gives **157.3 mm unladen and 110.1 mm fully loaded**, plus mm figures that match the US sheet: 4556 / 1752 / 1435 / 2670 mm, track 1499 / 1522 mm ([hnca13]). [HONDA]
- Auto123 (Canadian LX sedan) gives 4556 × 1752 × 1436 mm, wheelbase 2670 mm, ground clearance 157 mm and a 10.8 m turning circle ([auto123]). [SINGLE, agrees with Honda]

**2012 Civic Sedan LX** [HONDA] ([hn12])
- Length **177.3 in** (4503 mm), width 69.0 in, height 56.5 in, wheelbase 105.1 in, track 59.0 / 59.9 in, curb weight LX automatic 2705 lb.
- **What this means:** the 2013 refresh made the car **2.1 in (≈53 mm) longer**, entirely from the new front and rear fascias. Wheelbase, track, height, width, doors, roof and glass are unchanged.

**2013 refresh: what changed (sedan only; the coupe was unchanged for 2013)** [HONDA press text, quoted at [conceptcarz] and [newswire-ca]]
- Front: restyled front and rear; a taller, more faceted hood; a black honeycomb mesh grille; an "open-mouth" lower bumper with a horizontal chrome accent; clear-lens corner lights; integrated fog lights on EX-L and above only.
- Rear: a new trunk lid; taillights that now continue onto the trunk lid; a new rear bumper, with a horizontal chrome trim piece across the lid; reflectors built into the rear bumper; a new lower diffuser panel with a honeycomb mesh vent.
- Wheels: restyled on all models.
- Wikipedia summarizes it as a U-shaped honeycomb grille with chrome accents replacing the 2012 three-bar grille, a new hood, new taillight lenses with additional clusters, and a chrome bar on the trunk lid ([wiki9]). [SINGLE, agrees with Honda]
- Headlamp housing shape: aftermarket replacement headlights are sold as fitting **"2012–2015 Civic Sedan"** ([auxito-hl]). This suggests the headlamp opening and shape carried over and only the corner lens changed to clear. [SINGLE, inference]

**Aerodynamics** (no absolute Cd published for the US sedan)
- Honda says the 2012 car has a "3.4 percent lower coefficient of drag compared to the 2011 Civic" ([hn12ov]). [HONDA]
- The 2016 press kit says the 10th gen has 12% less drag than the 9th gen but gives no Cd value ([hn16body]).

**Overhangs and angles:** No source gives front or rear overhang, approach angle or departure angle for the US sedan. The 2016 press kit says only that the 10th gen front overhang is "1.4 in shorter" and the rear overhang "2.9 in longer" than the 2015 car ([hn16body]); it gives no absolute values. An estimate is in §2.

## 2. Side-profile key points for modeling

> **Superseded by [exterior-shape.md](exterior-shape.md)**, which measures these points from reference photos. The two files differ by about 30 mm in how the 1887 mm of overhang splits between front and rear (930/957 here, 900/986 there); both are estimates.

**No usable blueprint was available.** The 2012–15 US sedan side elevations at getoutlines ([go-2059]) and the-blueprints are paywalled. **Every number in this table except the Honda ones is an [EST]**, built from Honda's overall dimensions, the computed tire size, Honda's design description, and typical compact FWD sedan proportions. Expected error is about ±50 mm for heights and ±80 mm for positions along the car. A tape measure on a real car would firm these up (checklist at the end of this section).

Coordinate system: x = mm rearward from the front bumper's most forward point; z = mm above the ground; unladen car.

| Point | x (mm) | z (mm) | Conf. / basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front bumper, most forward point | 0 | ~480 | EST |
| Front bumper / air-dam lower edge | ~120 | ~180 | EST (lowest underbody point is 157 mm per [hnca13]) |
| Front wheel center | **~930** | **~305** | EST for x. z = computed tire radius 317 mm minus about 12 mm static deflection |
| Hood leading edge (top of grille) | ~120 | ~760 | EST |
| Hood rear edge / cowl / windshield base | ~1500 | ~1000 | EST. Honda: A-pillars "moved further forward", "steeper, more aggressive windshield angle" ([hn12ov]) |
| Windshield top (roof front header) | ~2400 | ~1395 | EST |
| **Windshield rake** | n/a | n/a | **~24° from horizontal** (≈66° from vertical), EST from the two points above |
| Roof peak (overall height) | ~2650 | **1435** | HONDA for height. x is EST; Honda: "highest roof point comes above the head of the front passengers" ([hn12ov]) |
| Roof rear / rear window top | ~3350 | ~1370 | EST |
| Rear window base / trunk-lid front edge | ~3950 | ~1075 | EST. Rear glass slope ≈ 26° from horizontal |
| Trunk deck, rear edge (top of trunk face) | ~4430 | ~1060 | EST. Deck ≈ 480 mm long, nearly flat |
| Rear wheel center | **~3600** | ~305 | front wheel x + 2670 wheelbase |
| Rear bumper, most rearward point | **4557** | ~560 | HONDA for x (overall length). z is EST |
| Rear bumper lower edge / diffuser | ~4450 | ~250 | EST |
| Beltline (side-window sill) | front door ~1000 → C-pillar ~1080 | n/a | EST. Honda: front beltline lowered for visibility ([hn12ov]) |
| Side character line | runs full length in the lower body | ~ wheel-arch top height | Honda: "a single character line runs the length of the side" ([hn12ov]) |
| Front / rear overhang | ~930 / ~957 | n/a | EST, constrained to sum to 1887 mm (Honda length − wheelbase) |

**Tire and wheel arch** (calculated from P195/65R15): diameter 634.5 mm, section width 195 mm, sidewall 126.75 mm, rim diameter 381 mm. Wheel-arch radius ≈ 340–350 mm [EST].

**Plan view:** body width 1752 mm [HONDA], which tapers at the bumper corners. Honda R&D says the bumper corners "project outward for a wider look" ([hrd-tr]). The track figures put the tire centerlines at ±749.5 mm (front) and ±761 mm (rear) from the car's centerline [derived from HONDA].

**Front view:** greenhouse (side glass) tumblehome is noticeable. Roof width ≈ 1150–1250 mm [EST].

**Measure-it-yourself checklist** (to replace the estimates): hood front-edge height; cowl height at the windshield base; windshield base and top positions measured from the front bumper; distance from front bumper to front wheel center; distance from rear wheel center to rear bumper; trunk-deck height and length; bumper heights; mirror-to-mirror width.

## 3. Panel layout

- **Hood:** hinged at the **rear edge near the windshield**. That is the standard layout, and the manual's release sequence opens the hood from the front. The procedure: pull the release under the lower left of the dashboard, push up the latch lever "in the center of the hood", then take the support rod out of its clamp and fit it into the hood. So the hood uses a **prop rod**, not struts. Source: 2013 Civic Hybrid sedan manual ([om13h-261]), which uses the same 9th-gen sedan body. [HONDA (Hybrid manual); LX very likely identical]
- **Doors:** 4 doors, conventional front-edge hinges (sedan). The front door includes a fixed quarter window at the A-pillar. Honda R&D: "front quarter-window… positioned on the door side" ([hrd-tr]). The forwardmost base of the A-pillar is covered by black trim ([hn12ov]). [HONDA]
- **Door handles:** body-colored ([hn13]) [HONDA]. They are horizontal **pull-type (grab)** handles; the outer handle part 72181-TR0-A11xx is shared with the 2012–16 CR-V ([hpn-handle]). [SINGLE for style]
- **Trunk lid:** all-new for 2013 ([conceptcarz]). The LX trunk-lid part is 68500-TR3-A30ZZ ([hpn-trunk]). It uses gooseneck hinges with torsion ("torque") rods, which Honda lists as "Hinge, L. Trunk 68660-TR6-A00ZZ" and "Trunk torque rod 74871-TR6-A30ZZ" ([search listing][hpc-hinge]). [SINGLE, part names only]. The inner taillights, back-up lights and license-plate lights are serviced from inside the trunk lid lining, so they are **mounted in the lid** ([om14s-387], [om14s-388]). [HONDA]
- **Fuel door:** **left (driver's) rear quarter.** The 2013 Civic Hybrid sedan manual says to stop with the pump at the left rear of the vehicle, and puts the release handle "at the foot of the driver's seat" ([om13h-248]). [HONDA (Hybrid sedan, same body)] Supporting this, the left quarter-panel listing shows the fuel-filler adapter as a related part ([hpn-qpL]). Check the car itself to be sure.
- **Side mirrors:** body-colored, power-adjusted, **not heated** on LX (heated only on EX-L) ([hn13]) [HONDA]. They fold manually and have no turn-signal repeater on the 2012–13 LX ([goparts-mirror]) [SINGLE]. They are mounted on the door at the front window corner and described as "low-drag side mirrors" ([hn12ov]).
- **Antenna:** "Integrated rear-window antenna" ([hn13]). It is printed in the rear glass, so **there is no roof mast or shark fin on the LX**. [HONDA] Edmunds lists an "element antenna" ([edm]); Honda is treated as authoritative here.
- **Spoiler:** none on LX. Only the HF has a body-colored decklid spoiler ([hn13]). [HONDA]
- **Moonroof:** none on LX (EX and EX-L only) ([hn13]). [HONDA]

## 4. Front design (2013 refresh)

- **Upper grille:** black honeycomb mesh with a chrome surround or accent ([newswire-ca], [gcr]) [HONDA + SINGLE]. A "Chrome Grille Molding, 2013–2015 Civic Sedan (excl. Hybrid/Si)" is listed as fitting the **LX** ([goparts-grille]), so the LX does get the chrome grille trim. [SINGLE]
- **Lower intake:** "new open-mouth lower bumper with a horizontal chrome accent" ([conceptcarz]). Greencarreports: "a deeper front bumper grille" ([gcr]). [HONDA]
- **Fog lights:** **none on the LX** ([hn13], US: EX-L only) [HONDA]. The fog-lamp corners of the bumper have bezels on non-fog cars; a fog-light bezel 71117-TR3-A51 is listed for the 1.8 L sedan, LX included ([goparts-fog]). [SINGLE]
- **Headlights:** multi-reflector halogen with auto-off on the LX ([hn13]) [HONDA]. Clear-lens corner sections hold the amber-bulb turn and parking lamp ([conceptcarz]). The shape is a swept-back unit wrapping into the front fender; the housing appears to be shared across the 2012–15 sedan ([auxito-hl]).
- **Honda "H" badge:** centered in the upper grille [EST, from general knowledge of the design; not documented in text].
- **Bumpers:** body-colored ([hn12], [hnca13]). [HONDA]

## 5. Rear design (2013)

- **Taillights:** two-piece "jewel-like" clusters. Honda: "The all-new design of the taillights now carries into the trunk face" ([conceptcarz]) [HONDA].
  - The **outer** part is in the body (quarter panel) and holds the brake/tail (21/5 W), rear turn (21 W amber) and an LED rear side marker ([om14s-385]).
  - The **inner** part is on the trunk lid and holds a taillight (3 CP) and the back-up light (16 W) ([om14s-387]). [HONDA (2014 sedan manual; same exterior)]
- **Chrome:** a horizontal chrome trim bar across the trunk lid ([conceptcarz]). [HONDA]
- **License plate:** on the trunk lid. The plate lights are serviced from the trunk-lid lining ([om14s-388]). [HONDA (inferred from service procedure)]
- **Rear bumper:** new for 2013, with integrated reflectors and a lower diffuser with honeycomb mesh vent ([newswire-ca]). [HONDA]
- **Exhaust:** LX has a single exhaust. A chrome exhaust finisher is **Si only** ([hnca13]) [HONDA], so the LX tip is plain and tucked under the bumper. **Which side it is on: not confirmed** in any source found. Best estimate: the driver's (left) side, visible only from low angles [EST, verify on car].

## 6. LX wheels and tires

- **Wheel:** 15-inch steel with full plastic wheel covers ([hn13]). The Honda parts-catalog name of the 2013 LX wheel is "**Disk, Wheel (15X6 1/2J) (TPMS) (Topy)**", part 42700-TR3-A02 ([hpn-cover]); the Canadian press kit also says LX "15x6.5J steel wheels with covers" ([hnca13]). [HONDA] Conflicts: Edmunds says 15 × 6.0 ([edm]), and a used-parts listing for the same part number says 15×6 ([ahparts]). **6.5 in (165 mm) is used** because Honda says so.
- **Wheel cover:** Honda part **44733-TR3-A00**, fits 2013 2-door and 4-door LX ([hpn-cover]). [HONDA] Design: silver-painted plastic, **5 split-spoke**, **bolt-on** (held by the lug nuts) ([hubcaphaven], [tractorsupply]). [MULTI (aftermarket listings)]
- **Tire:** P195/65R15 89H all-season ([hn13]). [HONDA]
- **Bolt pattern / hub:** 5×114.3 mm, center bore 64.1 mm, M12×1.5 lug nuts, 108 N·m ([limitless], [koleso]). [MULTI]
- **Offset:** **not found** for the 15×6.5 steel wheel. Koleso lists ET45 for the 9th-gen sedan's OE 16-inch wheel ([koleso]). Best estimate for the LX steel wheel: **ET45 ±5** [EST].

## 7. Paint, glass and trim colors

- **LX sedan colors (US 2013)**, from Edmunds' LX sedan page: Urban Titanium Metallic, Taffeta White, Kona Coffee Metallic, **Alabaster Silver Metallic**, Crystal Black Pearl, Crimson Pearl, **Polished Metal Metallic**, Dyno Blue Pearl ([edm]). [SINGLE] The Honda 2013 brochure lists the same family of names for the Civic sedan and notes that not every color is offered on every trim ([brochure]). [HONDA]
- **Silver candidates and their codes** ([paintscratch13]) [SINGLE]:
  - **Alabaster Silver Metallic, NH-700M**: the bright, light silver. Most likely what most people call "silver."
  - **Polished Metal Metallic, NH-737M** (paintscratch calls it "Polished Gray Metal Metallic" and describes it as "a deep, balanced gray"): a dark gunmetal gray.
  - The color label in the driver's door jamb shows which one a given car has ([paintscratch13]).
- **Hex values:** no reputable source publishes an RGB or hex value for either paint. For rendering only [EST]:
  - Alabaster Silver base ≈ `#BEC2C5`, with metallic highlights ≈ `#E2E5E7` and shadow ≈ `#7D8186`
  - Polished Metal ≈ `#6A6D70`
  - Use a metallic or clear-coat material (metalness about 0.6–0.8, roughness about 0.3).
- **Body-colored on LX:** bumpers, door handles, mirror caps ([hn13], [hn12]). [HONDA]
- **Black trim** [EST except where cited]:
  - black A-pillar base trim ([hn12ov], HONDA)
  - black window surrounds and B-pillar appliqué
  - black lower grille and diffuser mesh ([conceptcarz], HONDA)
  - black wiper cowl
- **Glass:** no tint specification found. Assume the light green factory tint typical of Honda [EST]. No privacy glass.

## 8. Wipers

- **Blade lengths, OEM** [HONDA parts catalog] ([hpn-wiper]):
  - Driver **650 mm**, part 76620-TR0-A02, fits 2013 4-door LX
  - Passenger **575 mm**, part 76630-TR0-A02
  - The common aftermarket equivalent is 26 in + 22 in ([paul-wiper]) [SINGLE]
- Note: the coupe uses 700/600 mm and the Hybrid sedan uses a 700 mm driver blade, so do not copy those numbers.
- **Arms:** 2. Conventional hook-type blade attachment ([paul-wiper]).
- **Parking position:** both arms rest along the base of the windshield in the cowl area, both sweeping the same direction with the driver side longer. No source text found; this is based on the standard Honda layout [EST].

## Open items and uncertainties

1. **All side-profile points except length, height and wheelbase are estimates**, including overhangs, hood and cowl heights, and windshield rake. No free blueprint was available; [exterior-shape.md](exterior-shape.md) refines them from reference photos.
2. **Wheel width:** Honda's parts name says 15×6.5J; Edmunds says 15×6.0. Offset not found (ET45 estimated).
3. **Silver paint:** Alabaster Silver (NH-700M) and Polished Metal (NH-737M) are both offered. Check the door-jamb label. No official hex values exist.
4. **Exhaust tip side:** unconfirmed.
5. **Headlamp bulb types** come from the 2013 coupe manual plus retailers. The sedan manual's headlamp page was unreadable.
6. **Fuel door (left) and hood prop rod** are confirmed from the 2013 Civic Hybrid sedan manual, which uses the same body, not the LX manual itself.
